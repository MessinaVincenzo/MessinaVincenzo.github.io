{%- comment -%} Reward-based selective propagation, drawn as a figure rather than decoration. This is the mechanism behind the Collaborative Satellite Network papers: each satellite scores an observation opportunity with a reward function, and only the satellite ID and the best score travel across the network - never the imagery. The animation walks that comparison outward hop by hop and settles on the winner. Pure inline SVG + CSS. No script, so it is safe under HTML minification, and every animation is suspended under prefers-reduced-motion. {%- endcomment -%}
