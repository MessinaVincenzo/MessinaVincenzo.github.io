EventSat is a student-driven mission aimed at demonstrating space-based
detection, classification, and identification of objects by leveraging
neuromorphic vision. The mission consists of a 6U CubeSat with
commercial-grade subsystems and a payload comprising an event camera and a
commercial-off-the-shelf embedded AI computer. As part of the mission,
algorithms are developed for event compression and object detection, taking
into account the camera’s very low latency and the specific characteristics of
event data. The data may inform space objects catalogues for Space Situational
Awareness (SSA). The EventSat mission serves as a platform for both academic
and educational activities, allowing for the research and development of novel
vision technologies and algorithms for their application in space, as well as
providing training opportunities for students through hands-on learning with
real space hardware. In this paper, we provide insights into the student-based
structure of the team and the educational projects associated with it, with a
focus on the impact on student learning through their involvement in the
mission.
