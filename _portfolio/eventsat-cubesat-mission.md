**Role:** Design Lead, ADCS and Thermal Control &middot; 2023 - Present

EventSat is a 6U CubeSat mission for object detection in space using event cameras.

I serve as design lead for two subsystems:

* **Attitude Determination and Control (ADCS)** - pointing and attitude control
  design for the observation payload.
* **Thermal Control** - thermal design and analysis for the spacecraft.

I also support project management for the mission.

Related publication: [Leveraging Event-Based Cameras for Enhanced Space Situational
Awareness: A Nanosatellite Mission Architecture Study](/publication/event-based-cameras-space-situational-awareness),
75th International Astronautical Congress, 2024.
