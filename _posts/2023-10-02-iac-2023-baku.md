In Baku I presented *"Initial Formulation of a Time Varying Dynamic Graph
Decentralized Optimization Framework for Scaled Satellite Network Infrastructure
Operations"* (with A. Golkar) &mdash; the starting point for the decentralized
coordination work that has run through my doctorate ever since.

I also co-authored *"Schedule Optimization for a Heterogeneous Earth Observation
Satellite Constellation"* (F. Strasser, V. Messina, A. Golkar, V. Dornauer),
presented at the same congress.
