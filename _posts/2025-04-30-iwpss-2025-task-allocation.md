I presented *"Efficient and Responsive Task Allocation in Distributed Satellite
Systems: The Role of Central Nodes"* (with A. Golkar) at the Fourteenth
International Workshop on Planning &amp; Scheduling for Space (IWPSS) in
Toulouse, France.

![Presenting at IWPSS 2025 in Toulouse](/images/iwpss-2025-toulouse.jpg)

The talk covered how central nodes can make task allocation across a distributed
satellite system both more efficient and more responsive.
