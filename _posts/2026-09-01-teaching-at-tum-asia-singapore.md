I spent two weeks in Singapore lecturing the exercise part of the **Space Mission
Design** course at TUM Asia, together with Prof. Alessandro Golkar.

With the students we worked through two mission studies: a **data-centre
constellation** and a **wildfire-monitoring constellation** for observing
Southeast Asia. Both are timely &mdash; the haze and wildfires across the region
on one side, the growing interest in space-based data centres on the other.

Thanks to the students for the effort and enthusiasm they put in, and to TUM Asia
for making it possible. Once again I found the beauty of learning through
teaching.
