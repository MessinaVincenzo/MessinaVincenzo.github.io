I served as **Vice Chair for Technical and Scientific Activities** of the 5th
**Symposium on Space Educational Activities**, co-organised by ESA and TUM and
held in Munich on 8&ndash;10 April 2026.

It took over a year of work: planning the symposium, selecting the technical
session topics, defining the review criteria, and staying in contact with
reviewers and authors from all over the world. On the days themselves the list
ran from moving tables and checking microphones to **chairing the satellite
mission session** &mdash; the only session I have chaired to date.

I also had the pleasure of **introducing the keynote talk by Paolo Ferri**, which
earned a very long applause at the end.

The symposium brought together around 300&ndash;350 students, researchers and
industry representatives. What I valued most was giving students and early-career
professionals a venue to share their space research, and seeing how programmes
and student groups around the world are making the space ecosystem stronger.

It was demanding, but the organising team carried each other through it. Thanks
to Gisela Detrell, Ramón García Alarcia and Luisa Metten for chairing the
symposium, and to everyone on the team.

Our EventSat mission paper appears in the symposium proceedings. I am a
co-author on it; it was presented by colleagues, not by me &mdash;
[read the paper](/publication/eventsat-cubesat-neuromorphic-vision-mission).
