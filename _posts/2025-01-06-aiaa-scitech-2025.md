I presented *"Advancing Federated Satellite Systems Performance: A Collaborative
Method for Improved Object Detection in Space"* (with A. Golkar) at the AIAA
SciTech Forum 2025 in Orlando, USA.

![Presenting at AIAA SciTech 2025 in Orlando](/images/aiaa-scitech-2025-orlando.jpg)

The work looks at how satellites in a federation can collaborate to improve
object detection in space beyond what any single spacecraft achieves alone.
