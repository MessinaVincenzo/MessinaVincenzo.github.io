I spent two days at the TUM Akademiezentrum Raitenhaslach on a research
retreat with the group, working through the **EventSat** 6U CubeSat mission and
our computer-vision research directions.

EventSat is our technology demonstration mission applying event cameras to
resident space object detection &mdash; the thread that connects several of the
papers on this site.
