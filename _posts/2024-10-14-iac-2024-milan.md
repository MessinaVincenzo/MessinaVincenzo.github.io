My busiest congress so far. In Milan I presented *"Latency Optimization in
Centralized and Decentralized Coordination of Time-Varying Scaled Satellite
Networks: The Impact of Data Size"* (with A. Golkar), and co-authored four
further papers across the symposia:

* [Leveraging Event-Based Cameras for Enhanced Space Situational Awareness](/publication/event-based-cameras-space-situational-awareness) &mdash; our EventSat mission architecture study
* [Trade Space Analysis and Conceptual Design for a Lunar Navigation and Communication Constellation](/publication/tradespace-analysis-lunar-navigation-constellation)
* [Advancing Digital Twin Implementation for CubeSats](/publication/advancing-digital-twin-implementation-cubesats)
* [Orbital Maneuvering Optimization Techniques for Cooperative Formation Flight and Collision Avoidance](/publication/orbital-manoeuvring-optimization-collision-avoidance)
