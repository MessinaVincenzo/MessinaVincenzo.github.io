*"Advancing Satellite Network Performance: Network Analysis for Federated
Satellite Systems"* is out in **IEEE Access**, with S. Scrocciolani,
R. M. García Alarcia, J. Sindermann and A. Golkar.

The paper quantifies how nominal satellite operations and inter-satellite link
assumptions shape the behaviour of a federated satellite network, using a
distributed simulator built on the IEEE 1516-2010 standard. Even under poor
on-board resource availability, the federation holds up as a self-sufficient,
decentralized paradigm &mdash; including for a rapid-mapping request after a
natural disaster.
