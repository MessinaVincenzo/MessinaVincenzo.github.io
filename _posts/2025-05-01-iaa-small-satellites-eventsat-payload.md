Our paper *"Design and Analysis of an Event Camera Payload for Space-Based
Object Detection on the EventSat 6U CubeSat Mission"* went to the 15th IAA
Symposium on Small Satellites for Earth System Observation, led by S. Dolan and
L. Schuberth with myself and the rest of the EventSat team.

It estimates the limiting apparent visual magnitude for the Prophesee EVK4
sensor, relates resident space object speed, brightness and size to the optic's
f-number and contrast threshold, and works through a single-stage baffle design
for rejecting stray light.
