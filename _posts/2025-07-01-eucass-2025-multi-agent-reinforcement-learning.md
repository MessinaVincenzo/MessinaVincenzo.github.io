*"Autonomous Decision-Making for Satellite Systems: A Decentralized
Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach in Partially Observable Dynamic
Environments"* appeared at the European Conference for Aerospace Sciences
(EuCASS), with C. J. Juan Oliver, S. Dolan and A. Golkar.

The learned policies match or outperform centralized rule-based and mixed-integer
programming benchmarks for a 20-satellite Space Situational Awareness
constellation, and generalize across coordination topologies despite being
trained fully decentralized. Inference on space hardware runs in the microsecond
range.
