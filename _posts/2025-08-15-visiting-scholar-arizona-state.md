From August to December 2025 I was a **Visiting Scholar at Arizona State
University**, hosted by Prof. Paul Grogan, working on identifying optimal levels
of centrality for collaborative satellite operations in distributed satellite
systems.

Two strands came out of it. I contributed to the **Tradespace Analysis Toolkit
for Constellations (TAT-C)**, implementing a decentralized task allocation
mechanism inside it. And I collaborated with **NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory**
on the **Observing System Simulation Experiments (OSSE)** project, using the
GEOS-5 Nature Run Data Portal to study planetary boundary layer perturbations.

I also presented the work to the Information Systems Engineering community at
ASU &mdash; see the [seminar talk](/talks/asu-decentralized-task-allocation).

The collaboration with ASU and with colleagues at JPL continues now that I am
back at TUM.
